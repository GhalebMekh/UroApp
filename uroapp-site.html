FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is UroApp for?

Urology residents preparing for Saudi and Arab Board exams, and practicing urologists who want validated bedside tools. It is built for licensed healthcare professionals, not patients.

Does it work offline?

Yes. In the iPhone app, the calculators, guideline summaries, and question bank are bundled on-device. Only the Evidence AI needs a connection, because it searches live sources.

How does the Evidence AI avoid made-up answers?

It is constrained to search current literature, attach a numbered citation to every claim, and state explicitly when evidence is conflicting or absent. It summarizes evidence for your judgment — it never issues patient-specific directives.

Is any patient data stored?

No. UroApp stores no patient-identifiable information. Calculator inputs stay on your device, and AI questions should never contain patient identifiers.
