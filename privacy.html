Privacy Policy

UroApp · effective 30 October 2026

UroApp is a clinical reference and training app for urologists and urology residents, published by Dr Ghaleb Al-Mekhlafi (Jeddah, Saudi Arabia) as an individual developer ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short, most of UroApp works entirely on your device. Nothing is collected unless you choose to create an account, and patient information must never be entered.

Without an account

  • Calculators, guidelines, drug information and every other reference tool run on your device. Inputs are not sent anywhere.
  • Your ward list, logbook, MDT case sheets, CME record, app-lock settings, favourites and rehearsal progress are stored only on your device.
  • We use no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.
  • Weekly reads and the conference list are downloaded as data files from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Like any website, GitHub receives your device's IP address when it serves those files. Links to guidelines, journals and conferences open those organisations' websites, which have their own privacy policies.

If you create an account (optional)

An account backs up some of your data and keeps it in step between your devices. We then store:

InformationWhy
Email address and password (stored only as a secure hash)To sign you in and send sign-up and password-reset codes
Profile: display name, training level or role, institution (all optional)Shown in the app and printed on your own logbook and CME exports
Operative logbook entries (date, patient sex, procedure, your role, complication grade and note)Backup and sync
CME / CPD activities and your CME targetBackup and sync
Favourite and recently used calculatorsBackup and sync

Never uploaded: your ward list and MDT case sheets. They stay on your device even when you are signed in, and our database is configured to refuse them.

Camera and certificate photos: UroApp uses the camera, or your photo library, only when you choose to attach a photo of a CME certificate. The photo is stored on your device only; it is never uploaded or synced, and it is deleted when you remove it or the activity it belongs to.

Account data is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany) region, and sign-up and reset emails are sent from our support mailbox through Google (Gmail), so Google handles your email address and the code we send. These providers process the data only on our behalf. Each signed-in user can access only their own data; this is enforced by the database itself.

Patient information

UroApp is designed so that no patient-identifiable information is needed. Do not enter patient names, record numbers, dates of birth or other identifiers anywhere in the app, including free-text notes. You are responsible for complying with your institution's rules and applicable law when recording clinical activity.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment details. To know which features you have unlocked, the app receives your subscription status and purchase history from Apple through our subscription service provider (RevenueCat). RevenueCat knows you by an anonymous app identifier, or, if you are signed in to an account, by your account's user ID, so that a subscription also unlocks your other devices. It does not receive your name or email address.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Account data is kept until you delete your account.
  • More → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account, profile and cloud backup straight away. Copies in our provider's routine backups are removed in line with its standard backup cycle.
  • Data on your device stays there until you delete it or delete the app. Sign out → remove from this device clears it from that device.

Your rights

Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law and similar laws, you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, which you can also do yourself by deleting your account. Contact support.uroapp@gmail.com.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). The optional app lock (PIN or Face ID) protects your ward list, logbook, MDT sheets and CME record from someone else using your phone. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

UroApp is for healthcare professionals and is not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
