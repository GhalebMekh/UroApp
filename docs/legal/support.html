UroApp Support

Questions, a problem, or a clinical error to report? Email support.uroapp@gmail.com. Please don't include any patient information.

Common questions

Restore a subscription on a new phone or iPad. More → Subscription → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Cancel a subscription. iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → UroApp.

Forgot the app-lock PIN. On the lock screen, tap "Forgot PIN?". This removes the lock and erases the locked data on that device. If you are signed in to an account, your logbook, CME record and favourites come back from the backup; the ward list and MDT sheets are never backed up.

Delete your account. More → Account → Delete account.
