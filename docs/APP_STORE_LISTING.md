## Description (4000)

> UroApp is a clinical reference and training tool for urologists and urology residents. Every
> score, threshold and summary cites its primary source with a link, and the clinical content
> works offline — on the ward, in theatre or in the basement.
>
> CALCULATORS
> 24 validated scores, each linked to its original publication: PSA toolkit (density, velocity,
> doubling time, free/total ratio), UCSF-CAPRA and CAPRA-S, ISUP Grade Group, EORTC NMIBC risk,
> IMDC, R.E.N.A.L. and PADUA nephrometry, adrenal incidentaloma, IGCCCG, TNM quick staging, IPSS,
> OABSS, IIEF-5, Guy's and STONE scores, ureteral stone passage, semen analysis (WHO 2021), free
> testosterone, eGFR, Clavien–Dindo and Charlson. Pin the ones you use most to your Home screen.
>
> GUIDELINES
> 27 conditions, oncological and benign, summarised in our own words from the AUA and EAU
> guidelines, each step linking to the official guideline page.
>
> ON CALL
> The first 30 minutes for testicular torsion (with the TWIST score), ischaemic priapism,
> Fournier's gangrene, the obstructed infected kidney and genitourinary trauma (AAST grading).
>
> OPERATIVE REFERENCE AND TRAINING
> 20 operations, from TURP and PCNL to pyeloplasty and hypospadias repair: anatomy, steps,
> complications, pre-operative work-up and set-up, danger zones with bail-outs, and
> post-operative care. Rehearse them by ordering the steps, choosing what comes next, and naming
> the structure at risk — with progress tracking and reminders when a procedure is due for a
> refresh. Plus an intra-operative decision scenario.
>
> PEDIATRIC UROLOGY
> 12 tools: UTD classification, hydronephrosis and reflux grading, UPJ obstruction, megaureter
> and valves, voiding dysfunction, nocturnal enuresis, UTI work-up, the acute scrotum, the
> undescended testis, GMS hypospadias score, maintenance fluids and antibiotic dosing.
>
> MORE
> • PI-RADS, Bosniak and VI-RADS with the management each category implies
> • Urology-relevant drug interactions, sorted by severity
> • Bilingual English–Arabic surgical consent for 18 operations, printable at the bedside
> • Weekly reads: new urology papers from PubMed, refreshed every week
> • Upcoming urology congresses, with calendar export
> • Operative logbook with Clavien–Dindo grading, surveillance schedules, ward list, MDT case
>   sheets and a CME / CPD tracker
>
> PRIVATE BY DESIGN
> No patient-identifiable data is collected. Calculator inputs never leave your phone, and the
> ward list and MDT sheets are never uploaded. Lock your private sections with a PIN or Face ID.
> An account is optional — it only backs up your own logbook, CME record and preferences.

## App Review notes (App Store Connect → App Review Information)

> UroApp is a reference and training tool for licensed urologists and urology residents. It does
> not diagnose or treat patients: every score and summary is decision support, cites its primary
> publication, and the app shows a medical disclaimer at first launch (also under More).
>
> No sign-in is needed — every feature can be reviewed without an account. The optional account
> (More → Account) only backs up the user's own logbook, CME record and preferences; sign-up
> sends a 6-digit code by email, so any reviewer address works. Account deletion is in-app:
> More → Account → Delete account.
>
> No patient-identifiable data is collected. The ward list and MDT sheets are stored on the
> device only and can be locked with a PIN / Face ID (More → App lock).
>
> The app downloads two content files (this week's PubMed article list and a list of urology
> congresses) as JSON data; no code is downloaded.
>
> [Once subscriptions exist:] Paid features show a plan screen with Restore Purchases; Restore
> Purchases is also under More.
